Your places (up to 5)

    Click the map to add a place, such as a workplace or a school, or : two workplaces and a weekend park.

    Weights set how much each place counts; trips per week work well (work 5, gym 2). The percentage is each place's share of the average. Drag a pin to move it.

    That's five: remove one to add another.
